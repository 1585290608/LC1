# Diesel fuel and dispatch

This study compares four islanded dispatch strategies and two fuel formulations over 24, 72 and 168 hours. The strategies vary surplus charging and generator commitment.

Start with the [original Chinese-language report](research-report.md). Its historical identifiers and internal paths refer to the original package. The files below are the selected copies. The [source map](../../docs/source-map.csv) records the originals.
