Important note
These figures are a presentation breakdown of the existing modelled Critical tier:
- Critical 0 = 25 kW command & communications (24 h/day)
- Critical 1 = 6 kW C4ISR cooling + 4 kW medical refrigeration (24 h/day)
- Critical 2 = 6 kW security lighting (12 h/day)

This is intended for dissertation presentation and comparison.
