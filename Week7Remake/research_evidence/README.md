On 21 November 2026, the original LC1 Sennybridge climate reference pack was imported with ZIP SHA-256 and CRC verified. Its four files match the package byte for byte, including UTF-8 BOMs, original CSV precision and line endings. The ZIP stays with the downloaded archive. ZIP SHA-256 `6917fdec14c370fddb017d226fb2758da81392dc0bf6c6044eab38809b3d46a0`. The verified station URL ends in `gcm2jvb2g`. All 12 months match the official 1991–2020 table for mean daily maximum and minimum temperature, air-frost days, rainfall, days with at least 1 mm rainfall and 10 m mean wind. Sunshine is unavailable. The official annual row reports 12.19 °C mean daily maximum, 4.79 °C mean daily minimum, 63.64 air-frost days, 1,565.62 mm rainfall, 185.27 days with at least 1 mm rainfall and 6.83 knots mean wind. Independently summing the monthly frost, rainfall and wet-day entries gives the same annual values for those three quantities. Monthly temperatures are midpoints of the published max/min means. The annual midpoint is 8.49 °C, and weighting the monthly midpoints by the days of the 2025 calendar gives 8.492356 °C. Both are proxies. Wind conversion uses 0.5144444444 m/s per knot. The annual wind normal converts to 3.513656 m/s.
