# WP5/T18 - Provisional Dissertation Methodology Notes

1. **Historical baseline (S0 site):** Import the pre-existing LC1 repository unchanged, including its 2025 hourly Sennybridge weather and 1,104 kWh/day synthetic load. Run inherited Python regression and register SHA-256 of original files. Distinguish 'S0 Original LC1 site' from the legacy controller **S0** (on-demand diesel dispatch).
2. **Revised design basis (S1 site):** Represent a proposed 120-person Operational Support Camp, with equipment-list-based 8,760-hour C4ISR and auxiliary demand, plus vehicle-specific 8,760-hour EV charging and 61,320 vehicle-hours of SOC records per scenario. Derive low/central/high loads from explicitly listed duty, utilisation, vehicle trip and charger assumptions.
3. **Hourly load identities:** At `dt=1 h`, `E_kWh = P_kW * 1 h`. For infrastructure, `P_i,t = installed_rated_kW_i * utilisation_case_i * scheduled_indicator_i,t`. For EV traction, `E_trip_kWh = mission_km * kWh_per_km`; charging input energy equals `DC_SOC_increment_kWh/eta_charge`.
4. **Dispatch simulation:** Feed new C4ISR/aux/EV arrays directly into the original S3 Python energy model; retain same PV/wind normalised weather, diesel fuel formulation, initial conditions, 100 kW DG, 90 kW PCS, 30% reserve floor, 70% starting SOC and 2,000 L fuel stock for comparable base runs. Inherited 25 kW dump sink and AC grid-forming reference remain model assumptions.
5. **Capacity screen:** PV and wind simultaneously selected at 50/75/100 kW, and BESS at 300/450/600 kWh. Keep 100 kW DG and 90 kW PCS fixed to avoid crossing uncalibrated DG unit classes. This nine-option screen does not establish a global optimum, full design-space Pareto or HOMER Pro result.
6. **Outage comparisons:** Fix the first 24, 72 and 168 hours of the same January 2025 LST source profile; initialise each independently, preventing carryover of fuel/SOC. Report Critical Load EA, tier-specific ENS, fuel use, ending SOC and full-system conservation. Do not infer EENS without outage probability data.
7. **Model exception quarantine:** If S3 dispatch creates a same-hour BESS charging/discharging conflict, stop and mark the candidate unavailable for ranking; do not auto-upsize or silently alter physics. Test controller alternatives only in separate versioned experiments.
8. **Cost boundary:** Use inherited illustrative GBP cost factors to compare candidate upfront CAPEX only. Exclude claims about 20-year NPC, tariffs, export income, equipment life, or levelised costs without running the full reworked economic model.
9. **EV power support:** During present W7 runs, EVs are *loads only* and provide **0 kW emergency discharge**. Future T11–T14 require OEM-backed bidirectional chargers, islanded grid forming, mission SOC and live vehicle availability; all future simulations must avoid treating theoretical V2G as installed reality.
10. **Validation hierarchy:** SOURCE_VERIFIED (unchanged SHA) → SOFTWARE_VERIFIED (tests/repeatability) → ENERGY_ACCOUNTING_VERIFIED (conservation and constraints) → NATIVE_MDT_CROSSCHECKED (matched solver case) → PHYSICAL_ELECTRICAL_VERIFIED (not achieved). Do not collapse these levels into a single 'validated' label.

All outputs here are **research-stage deterministic estimates**, not evidence of a physical system deployed at a defence site. Any use in a university dissertation should disclose code generation and analysis assistance according to applicable Cardiff University guidance.
